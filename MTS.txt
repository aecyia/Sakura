[list][*] Download. Extract the file.
[*] Move to "Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\Mods\"
[*] Can be placed in Overrides OR Packages
[*] Play + Enjoy ♡
[*] Have an idea / suggestion? Found a visual issue? Send feedback via the comments section, or [url=https://github.com/aecyia/Sakura/issues]here[/url]. I use this to monitor issues and changes. It will always have the full history, previous release versions, and the latest updates. This will be checked more often than ModTheSims![/list]

[b][i][u]Warnings[/u][/i][/b]
[list][*] Only use one recolour mod at a time! This will conflict with any other mod that changes the UI image assets. You can prioritize via "Resource.cfg" in your Mods folder.
[*] The colours may appear different based on your computer’s brightness and colour settings. Especially when taking screenshots, it may come out as a super dark pink.[/list]
