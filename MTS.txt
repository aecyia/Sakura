Tired of the blue UI ?

Want a light pink one ?

This project aims to recolour the UI, redesign icons, and more ♡

[u][i][b]Installation[/b][/i][/u]
[list][*] Download. Extract the file.
[*] Move to "Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 3\Mods\"
[*] Can be placed in Overrides OR Packages
[*] Play + Enjoy ♡[/list]

[b][i][u]Warnings[/u][/i][/b]
[list][*] Only use one recolour mod at a time! This will conflict with any other mod that changes the UI image assets. If you have other mod packages that changes these, prioritize via "Resource.cfg" in your Mods folder.
[*] The colours may appear different based on your computer’s brightness and colour settings. Especially when taking screenshots, it may come out as a super dark pink.[/list]
